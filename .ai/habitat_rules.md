# Habitat Rules

## Focus

- Evidence-derived capability claims
- Third-person resume representation
- Observation → inference → hypothesis → falsification
- Provenance and implementation evidence
- Contradictory evidence and alternative explanations

## Out of Scope

- Job-fit evaluation
- Personality classification
- Psychological diagnosis
- Self-promotion without evidence
- Treating conceptual architecture as implemented fact

## Evidence Quality

- HIGH: directly inspectable implementation, artifact, or repeated documented behavior
- MEDIUM: repeated pattern with reasonable inference but incomplete independent validation
- LOW: plausible mechanism, analogy, or internal-cognition claim without direct access

## Style

World Slice first. Keep observations separate from mechanisms. Label confidence explicitly. Preserve uncertainty.
